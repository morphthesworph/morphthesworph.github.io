Polygrams

Polygrams are parent object to polygons, down below are some of my notes on them.

What are polygrams?

Like I said before, polygrams encapsulates polygons; what makes a polygram not a polygon is self-intersection and the fact that a polygram can consist of multiple sets of edges that don't need to intersect. The most famous examples of this are the pentagram and star of david.
